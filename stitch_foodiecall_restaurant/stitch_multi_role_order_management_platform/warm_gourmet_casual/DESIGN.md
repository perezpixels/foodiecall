## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the experience of an approachable yet curated modern culinary kitchen. It balances tactile warmth with contemporary digital fluidity, evoking the sensory pleasures of fresh ingredients, chef-crafted balance, and effortless dining.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Target Audience:** Urban epicures, busy professionals, and discerning food lovers who crave quality, wholesome culinary preparation without formal stiffness.
- **Tone:** Inviting, mouth-watering, reliable, and refined casual.
- **Visual Stance:** A blend of culinary warmth and modern utility. It avoids cold tech minimalism in favor of sun-baked terracotta, crisp herb greens, creamy porcelain backgrounds, and soft, touch-friendly architecture.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Culinary Warmth:** Highlighting warm neutrals, rich natural food accents, and sun-bathed card surfaces that make food imagery pop.
- **Organic Softness:** Ample corner radiuses, cushioned pill containers, and warm ambient diffusion rather than sharp, severe edges.
- **Tactile Ergonomics:** Minimum 48px touch targets, deep contrast text hierarchies for mobile viewing in shifting daylight, and distinct layered surfaces that invite thumb-driven exploration.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by appetizing culinary pigments complemented by natural stoneware neutrals. This system operates exclusively in light mode to maintain the clean, bright ambiance of sunlit kitchens and natural culinary photography.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#EA580C`):** Vibrant Terracotta/Tangerine. Used for core interactive elements, checkout buttons, primary active states, and appetizing hero highlights.
- **Secondary (`#16A34A`):** Botanical Basil Green. Denotes dietary tags (vegan, vegetarian, organic), sustainability badges, order fulfillment statuses, and fresh culinary accents.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Muted Golden Amber. Used for star ratings, loyalty milestones, chef specials, and limited-time offer flags.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1C1917`):** Deep Charcoal. The bedrock for titles, high-emphasis text, and core icons, ensuring crisp contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#44403C`):** Warm Slate. Applied to secondary body text, modifiers, descriptions, and passive icon states.
- **Background Base (`#FAFAF9`):** Warm Porcelain. The foundational canvas surface that imparts organic warmth behind content cards.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp White. Applied to elevated menu cards, bottom sheets, sticky bars, and modals.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#F5F5F4`):** Soft Warm Stone. Used for input fields, quantity counter backgrounds, tag chips, and card recesses.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E7E5E4`):** Warm Oat Outline. Applied at 1px thickness for structural borders and dividers without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to produce an open, friendly, and structured aesthetic.

### Application Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Set tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) with bold or extra-bold weights to convey editorial authority and culinary mastery.
- **Body:** Generous line heights ensure ingredients, dish backstories, and customization options remain scannable on high-density displays.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or sentence-case chips and price indicators utilize semibold and bold weights with subtle positive tracking for instantaneous readability under mobile arm’s-length conditions.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Always render monetary values and calorie metrics with semibold or bold weights to ensure immediate visual anchoring during basket compilation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first utility, using an 8pt spatial grid system to deliver natural rhythm, tactile rhythm, and thumb-friendly scanning.

### Layout System
- **Mobile Foundation:** 4-column fluid layout with an outer canvas margin of `16px` (`margin`) and column gutters of `16px` (`gutter`).
- **Tablet / Large Mobile Fold:** Extends to 8 columns with `24px` outer canvas margins and `16px` gutters.
- **Safe Area Insets:** All top headers reserve a minimum of `44px` safe margin for dynamic hardware islands; bottom floating actions account for `34px` home indicator clearance plus `16px` layout padding.

### Spacing Tokens in Action
- **`space-xs` (4px):** Micro gaps between icons and adjacent labels, badge internal vertical padding.
- **`space-sm` (8px):** Tag chip spacing, dish tag clusters, compact item row dividers.
- **`space-md` (16px):** Primary component inner padding (cards, text input boxes, list rows), vertical stack distance between related form controls.
- **`space-lg` (24px):** Separation between distinct menu categories, card margins, bottom sheet content padding.
- **`space-xl` (32px):** Top-level section transitions, hero header padding, confirmation interstitial gutters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through warm-tinted ambient shadows and subtle tonal layering, abandoning harsh drop-shadows in favor of a soft, appetizing glow.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAFAF9` warm porcelain canvas. Base backdrop for scrolled views.
2. **Level 1 (Inlaid Surfaces):** `#F5F5F4` warm stone. Sits flush with 1px `#E7E5E4` outlines; applied to search bars, stepper counters, and inactive filters.
3. **Level 2 (Cards & Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` crisp white with soft ambient shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.03);`
   - Bound with a subtle 1px border of `#E7E5E4` to separate overlapping food photography from canvas backgrounds.
4. **Level 3 (Floating Bars & Cart Trays):** Sticky bottom elements and quick-action navigation bars:
   - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.06);`
   - Backgrounds feature `92%` opaque `#FFFFFF` coupled with a `16px` backdrop blur for tactile translucency.
5. **Level 4 (Sheets, Modals & Menus):** Customization drawers and checkout panels:
   - `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.16);`
   - Backed by an ambient scrim of `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.45)` with `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The shape system employs generous, organic curvature (`roundedness: 2`) that conveys friendliness and a modern bistro feel.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (`rounded-md`, 8px):** Compact tags, dietary flags, segmented controls.
- **Standard Interactive Elements (`rounded-lg`, 16px):** Form fields, list item rows, minor callouts.
- **Feature Elements (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`, 20px–24px):** Menu food cards, bottom sheets, promotional banners, modal dialogs.
- **Touch Pillars (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Primary CTAs, add-to-bag buttons, pill chips, floating back buttons, and counter steppers.
- **Images:** All item thumbnail images feature a minimum radius of `16px`, mirroring card geometry for unified softness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `52px` (minimum `48px` touch target), background `#EA580C`, label in crisp white `label-lg`. Active scale micro-interaction (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Height `48px`, background `transparent`, 1.5px border `#EA580C`, text `#EA580C` `label-lg`.
- **Botanical / Action Pill:** Background `#16A34A`, text `#FFFFFF`, used for order reordering and green initiative actions.
- **Subtle Action Button:** Height `40px`, background `#F5F5F4`, text `#1C1917`, no border.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Active State:** Background `#1C1917`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded-full, `8px 16px` padding.
- **Inactive State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E7E5E4`, text `#44403C`, rounded-full.
- **Dietary Badges:** Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#16A34A`, `rounded-md`, `4px 8px` padding with `label-sm` font.

### Cards (Menu Items)
- Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, `20px` corner radius, 1px `#E7E5E4` perimeter stroke.
- Top or left-aligned food imagery with `aspect-ratio: 4/3` or `1:1`, cropped smoothly with matching inner border radius.
- Padding `16px` around description, bold price in `#1C1917`, and a bottom-right floating `+` button (`36px` circle in `#EA580C` with white glyph).

### Lists & Navigation Rows
- Minimalist edge-to-edge content container with 1px border-bottom `#F5F5F4`.
- Height: Minimum `56px` per row for thumb reach.
- Leading icon or thumbnail in a `40px` rounded square (`#F5F5F4`), trailing chevron in `#44403C`.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Height `52px`, background `#F5F5F4`, corner radius `16px`, text `#1C1917`, placeholder `#78716C`.
- Border transitions: Zero border in resting state, shifting to 1.5px solid `#EA580C` with a warm subtle focus glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.12)`) on focus.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radio Buttons:** `22px` diameter, 2px border `#D6D3D1`. Selected state uses `#EA580C` outer border with a centered `#EA580C` solid dot (`10px`).
- **Checkboxes:** `22px` rounded square (`6px` radius). Selected state displays `#EA580C` fill with a crisp white check icon.

### Quantity Stepper
- Compact pill container (`rounded-full`), height `36px`, background `#F5F5F4`.
- Plus/Minus icon hit areas at minimum `36x36px`, active state text `#1C1917` with bold count indicator in the center.

### Sticky Order Bar
- Fixed to bottom viewport, floating `16px` above safe area with `16px` side margins.
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, `24px` radius, warm shadow level 3, houses real-time total, item count, and full primary checkout trigger.
